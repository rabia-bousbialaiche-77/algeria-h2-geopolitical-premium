# Changelog

## 2.1.0 (2026-11-15) - revision after review

Model
* **PV clipping.** The annual cost of the PV plant is now divided by the electricity that reaches the electrolyser, (1 - 0.03) x yield, instead of the gross yield. The electrolyser full-load hours already used the clipped yield, so the two terms of the production cost now share one energy balance. Algerian production cost at central parameters and 8% WACC: 5.74 USD/kg (5.67 in v2.0.0).
* **Ammonia reference voyage.** The 10,000 km voyage subtracted from the IRENA chain cost is valued at the exporter's baseline (S0) production cost, without canal toll or war-risk insurance. The distance-independent conversion cost therefore no longer moves with the scenario WACC. The Algerian ammonia premium is now slightly above the pipeline premium (1.11 against 1.09 USD/kg in S3); in v2.0.0 it was slightly below.
* **New bounding variants** in `table_variants.csv`: midstream capital exposed to the exporter's WACC (upper bound), cargo used as fuel valued at production cost + 1 USD/kg, and an independent PV yield factor per site. Their parameters are author assumptions in `params/production*.yaml`.
* **Sobol table** lists all 13 inputs for each output with a status: active, structurally inactive, or no effect on the sampled support.
* Shapley output now reports the interaction term and the medians of the two parts (used in Fig. 3).
* `results_full.json` stores unrounded statistics for every variant; `summary.json` adds the central-parameter decomposition used in the article.

Data
* `data/raw/observed_2026.csv`: eight dated war-risk quotes added (Red Sea, 27-30 March, 13 April, 21 and 23 July 2026; Strait of Hormuz, 30 March, 10 April, 18 June 2026) from Argus Media, Insurance Business and Reuters via Insurance Journal. Scenario H26 is unchanged; its ranges already contain these quotes.

Figures and documents
* Fig. 1 redrawn with the notation of the article's equations. Fig. 3 notes the different vertical scales. Fig. 5(b) plots every dated quote with its range.
* `table_validation_2026.csv` rewritten with the new observations and explicit ratios for the financing comparison.
* Supplementary Material regenerated by `scripts/make_supplementary.py`.
* Equation numbers in the code comments follow the revised article (Eqs. 1-10).

Tests
* New tests for the clipping accounting, the invariance of the conversion cost, the midstream variant, the Shapley interaction and the headline numbers of the article.
